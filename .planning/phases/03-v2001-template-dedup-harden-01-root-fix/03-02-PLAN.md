# Plan 02: Migrate Complex Templates (Wave 2)

## Goal

After Wave 1 validates the macro approach on simple templates, migrate the 6 remaining complex templates: concat_delay, overlap_bitvec, nonoverlap, rep_consecutive, disable_iff_top, seq_concat_top. These have counter/bv/FSM logic. Verify full regression and golden parity.

## Requirements

- **REFACTOR-01** — All 11 templates use macros for always-block bodies
- **REFACTOR-03** — All 736 tests pass, SV byte-identical, V2001 behaviorally equivalent
- **HARDEN-01** — Fix verified in all templates (including bit-vector ones with `ant_pass_w` trigger)

<read_first>
- templates/concat_delay.sv.j2 (counter with cnt_width parameter, ##N and ##[M:N] modes)
- templates/_macros.sv.j2, templates/_attempt_fired_macro.sv.j2
- Wave 1 gate results (confirmed macro approach works)
</read_first>
<action>
Refactor concat_delay.sv.j2. Key differences from simple templates:

Apply macro refactoring: type declarations → signal_type, always header → always_block_header, attempt_fired_q → attempt_fired_logic with trigger="start". Counter logic body remains verbatim.

After refactor: verify SV golden byte-identical, then V2001 golden parity.
</action>

Apply macro refactoring to both templates. Nonoverlap is structurally similar to overlap but with an extra one-cycle delay register.
</action>
