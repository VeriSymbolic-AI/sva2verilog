# v2.0 Milestone Audit

## Verdict

**PASS for the declared v2.0 Open Formal Verification scope.** The milestone
delivers a bounded, fail-closed, open-source DUT formal-verification path for
the documented SVA subset, independently of the finite synthesizable-monitor
backend. It does not claim complete IEEE 1800 SVA support, JasperGold feature
equivalence, chip correctness, or industrial sign-off.

The executable audit baseline is
`e3526836912086fdc274528ca7735dd7b6a028e1`. It passed exact-SHA CI
`30908155956` (13/13 jobs), differential nightly `30908168285` (3/3 jobs), and
Full Formal `30908170695` (8/8 shards). The Linux open-liveness shard ran 17/17
tests with Super Prove and no skip; the open-user-DUT shard ran 75/75 tests with
no skip.

## Deferred Scope and Residual Risk

There are no critical gaps inside the declared milestone, but the following
limits remain intentional and must not be hidden by a green workflow:

- Open liveness supports selected Boolean shapes only and depends on a
  qualified Super Prove runtime for successful proof. Absence produces UNKNOWN.
- Multi-clock temporal composition, X/Z-dependent semantics, general locals,
  and unsupported nesting remain fail-closed or require explicit reviewed
  decomposition.
- A generated finite monitor is not a proof of an unbounded liveness property;
  a formal proof of the property does not define every monitor-interface output.
- The support matrix remains at zero Fully supported rows. Row-specific
  independent references, proof depth, CDC analysis, and industrial corpora
  are still required before promotion.
- Neither this milestone nor its mutation score replaces commercial sign-off,
  physical CDC/metastability analysis, or verification of an entire chip.

## Merge and Release Recommendation

The implementation can be merged and used as the bounded v2.0 formal-first
capability baseline. Claims must remain scoped to the documented property
shapes, models, assumptions, checkers, tools, and evidence hashes. Any later
executable or workflow change requires fresh exact-commit CI, nightly, and Full
Formal qualification.

The final documentation-only ledger HEAD is also required to pass those three
same-SHA workflows before handoff. That outcome belongs in the release handoff
rather than being back-written into this self-referential audit file.
